# Blanko — Brand Identity v1.0

## Core Identity
- **App name:** Blanko (pronounced BLAN-ko / بلانكو)
- **Name story:** Blank + Origin (brand-created meaning, not a dictionary etymology)
- **Core category:** Digital Whiteboard & Creative Workspace
- **Official tagline:** Where ideas take shape.
- **Supporting line (product descriptor):** Your digital space to think, sketch, and create.
- **Voice:** Calm, confident, concise, creative, practical

## Logo concept
The `O` is an open circular stroke; the blue dot is the origin/starting point of the idea. Do not add pen, bulb or brain glyphs. The icon alone uses the `O`, not the full wordmark. Use the included vector SVGs as canonical geometry. The custom wordmark is outlines, not live text.

## Brand colors
| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #15191F | Wordmark, text, default icons |
| Origin Blue | #4464F6 | Primary buttons, focus, selected state, point in `O` |
| Canvas | #FFFFFF | Drawing canvas |
| Soft Surface | #F7F8FA | Toolbars, secondary surfaces |
| Border | #E7EAF0 | Dividers and subtle outlines |
| Muted Text | #687386 | Secondary labels |

Keep the actual whiteboard canvas pure white in light theme; colored sketch tools are independent of brand UI blue. Avoid vivid multi-color gradients, decorative glass effects, and excessive blue panels.

## Type
- English interface: Inter (400 / 500 / 600 / 700)
- Arabic interface: IBM Plex Sans Arabic (400 / 500 / 600 / 700)
- Wordmark: approved outlined SVG, **never** retype with UI font.
- Prefer short action-oriented UI copy and calm hierarchy.

## App placement
- **Splash:** `O` icon or wordmark, no extra paragraphs; if presenting the tagline, keep it only on onboarding or marketing.
- **Home header:** compact `O` icon + Blanko wordmark (when room permits).
- **Canvas:** minimal floating toolbar and content-first layout; do not place the marketing slogan inside the creative space.
- **Icon:** `icons/blanko_app_icon_light_1024.png` or dark counterpart; verify platform masking and Android adaptive icon safe-zone.
- **Dark mode:** white `O`/wordmark on #15191F and same Origin Blue dot.

## Logo restrictions
1. Don't close the gap in `O`.
2. Don't change position/relative size of the blue dot.
3. Avoid filters, shadows, fake brush texture and gradients.
4. Keep adequate clearspace (at least diameter of the dot around standalone mark).
5. Use SVG master for scaling, PNG 4K for raster needs.

## Publishing checklist
`Blanko` is already used by unrelated apps on app stores. Before commercial launch check relevant trademarks, legal territories, domains, app title availability, and social handles. This is design identity, **not legal name clearance**.
